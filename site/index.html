THE IDEA

A conversation changes.
Its history should stay clear.

  1. 01 /

    Know what is current.

    Keep decisions, suggestions, assumptions, and open questions distinct.

  2. 02 /

    See what changed, and why.

    Trace a correction to the message behind it. Keep the earlier decision in its history.

  3. 03 /

    Take the context with you.

    Bring a compact, readable handoff to your next chat. Keep a full backup in an open format.

PLANNED FOR THE FIRST RELEASE

One project memory.
Across your tools.

A local command-line workflow for coding agents, plus a browser interface to review changes. Portable Markdown / JSON keeps the memory in your hands, with no application account or built-in API billing.
